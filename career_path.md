Based on your learning goals and the 6-month roadmap, here are some potential career paths, job titles, certifications, and platforms to explore:

**I. Potential Job Roles (Entry-Level):**

*   **Data Analyst:**
    *   **Description:** Analyzing data to identify trends, patterns, and insights to support business decisions. This aligns well with your focus on SQL, statistical analysis, and data visualization.
    *   **Where to look:** Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter.
    *   **Skills to Highlight:** SQL, Excel, Tableau/Power BI, statistical analysis, data cleaning, data wrangling, communication skills.
*   **Business Intelligence Analyst:**
    *   **Description:** Creating reports and dashboards to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and provide insights into business performance. Your planned Tableau skills are directly relevant.
    *   **Where to look:** Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter.
    *   **Skills to Highlight:** Data visualization (Tableau/Power BI), SQL, data warehousing concepts, communication skills, report writing.
*   **Marketing Analyst:**
    *   **Description:** Analyzing marketing data to optimize campaigns, improve customer engagement, and increase ROI. Your background as a Marketing Coordinator, combined with your new data skills, makes this a natural transition.
    *   **Where to look:** Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, company websites (marketing departments).
    *   **Skills to Highlight:** Data analysis, marketing knowledge, data visualization, SQL, A/B testing, campaign analysis.
*   **Junior Data Scientist:**
    *   **Description:** While a full Data Scientist role might be a stretch initially, some companies offer junior positions focused on specific tasks like data cleaning, feature engineering, or model evaluation.
    *   **Where to look:** Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, AngelList (startups).
    *   **Skills to Highlight:** Python, Pandas, basic machine learning knowledge, statistical analysis, data visualization.
*   **Data Analytics Consultant (Entry-Level):**
    *   **Description:** Some consulting firms hire entry-level analysts to assist with data-related projects for clients.
    *   **Where to look:** Consulting firm websites (Accenture, Deloitte, KPMG, etc.), LinkedIn.
    *   **Skills to Highlight:** Analytical skills, problem-solving, communication, adaptability, SQL, data visualization.

**II. Freelance Opportunities:**

*   **Data Visualization Specialist:**
    *   **Description:** Create dashboards and reports for clients using Tableau, Power BI, or other visualization tools.
    *   **Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal.
    *   **Portfolio:** Essential. Showcase your best visualizations and dashboards.
*   **SQL Developer/Analyst:**
    *   **Description:** Write SQL queries to extract, transform, and load data for clients.
    *   **Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, Guru.
    *   **Skills:** Strong SQL skills, experience with different database systems (e.g., MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server).
*   **Data Cleaning & Preparation:**
    *   **Description:** Help clients clean and prepare their data for analysis.
    *   **Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr.
    *   **Skills:** Experience with data cleaning techniques, Python (Pandas), and data wrangling.
*   **Report Automation:**
    *   **Description:** Automate report generation using tools like Python and scheduling tools.
    *   **Platforms:** Upwork, Freelancer.
    *   **Skills:** Python scripting, report generation, task scheduling.
